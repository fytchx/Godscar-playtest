# World and Progression — Current Direction
**Status:** Concept, no new game implementation authorized. Earlier measured Paladin mechanics are NOT proof of finished pacing.

## Three progression tracks
1. Character XP/levels unlock specialization points and improve character capability. Character levels DO NOT unlock weapon cast slots.
2. Equipment progression: weapon archetypes and curated found ability count, compatible imprints, affixes, rarity, upgrades, off-hand/armor.
3. World progression: region/hunt access, enemy-stage difficulty, boss and dungeon unlocks, recipe discoveries.

## Activity system
- Continuous hunting: main XP/gold/gear/material income; zone picker allows selecting one to four monsters in the same zone for automatic rotation.
- Target farming: each monster family has a mechanical threat and desirable drops.
- Major bosses: preparation checks, repeatable farming; first-clear recipe/progression rewards should be deterministic. Ordinary hunting isn't blocked forever by failure.
- Dungeons (proposed, not yet specified as content): bounded auto-run encounter sequence using SAME combat rules, one active combat timeline, no parallel farm duplication; themed equipment/material rewards; clear offline/resume behavior required.
- Contracts (proposed): optional no-expiration targets fulfilled during hunting, not daily chores.
- Codex: sources for monster, weapon archetype, material and recipes.
- Endgame (deferred): scalable challenge modifiers/depths; no mandatory sixth rarity/currency.

## Established named places/entities
Fallfields: existing starting zone. Current source enemy roster: Blighted Husk, Carrion Swarm, Grave Knight, Hollow Wisp; boss The Hollow Bellkeeper. Wisp fire resistance and Bellkeeper damage are the two planned progression walls. Grave Knight/Husk are NOT currently designated walls.
Ashen Hollow: existing second zone; Woundwarden boss concept in older prototype. Future locations (e.g. Thornwild, Stormbreak Coast) are world-design proposals, NOT approved lore/content.
Names for Fallfields sub-areas, dungeon locations, new bosses, first-clear rewards and exact difficulty gates remain open.

## Gameplay/design boundaries
Combat is fully automatic. Bosses challenge preparation, not reflex dodges. Failure should cost bounded time/resources, not irreversibly eliminate the ability to farm. Exact durations, level thresholds, dungeon drop rates, number of areas and stage progression require measured tests. Do not wire UI to simulation without owner authorization.
